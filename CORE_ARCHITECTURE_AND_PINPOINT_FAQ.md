## 1. Platform Core Identity & Fundamental Invariants

### 1.1 What is AgroSafe Travel?
AgroSafe Travel is an agrotourism booking and disaster-resilience platform specifically built for high-hazard rural terrains (Himalayas, Western Ghats, Coastal belts). It protects travelers both **physically** (via hyper-localized, multi-hazard risk evaluation and verified community alerts) and **financially** (via an escrow payment model, automated zero-fee weather rebooking, and atomic seat inventory locks).

### 1.2 The Three Non-Negotiable Architectural Invariants

#### Invariant 1: No "Black-Box" ML Model for Life Safety
* **The Rule**: Risk scores must **never** be generated by an uninterpretable, fine-tuned, or opaque neural network.
* **Why**: Machine learning models hallucinate, suffer from dataset distribution shift, and cannot explain *why* they produced a specific risk level. In disaster situations where human lives and travel cancellations are at stake, every single output must be deterministic, auditable, and backed by a transparent chain of evidence.
* **Implementation**: The risk engine runs on open, deterministic physical thresholds (e.g., IMD rainfall categories, GloFAS river discharge percentiles, NWS Heat Index equations). Every risk response ships with an explicit array of human-readable reasons and exact sensor timestamps.

#### Invariant 2: Official Government Alerts Set an Unbreachable Safety Floor
* **The Rule**: Our internal physical rules can **escalate** risk (e.g., from Green to Orange if local terrain slope and soil saturation warrant caution), but they can **never lower** an official government warning issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD), NDMA SACHET, Central Water Commission (CWC), or INCOIS.
* **Code Reference**: [`backend/src/services/riskEngine/fusion.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/riskEngine/fusion.ts#L131-L135):
  ```typescript
  const officialAlertFloor = Math.max(imdFloor, sachetFloor) as RiskLevelCode;
  // If government issued Orange (Level 2), the final score cannot be Green (Level 0) or Yellow (Level 1).
  ```

#### Invariant 3: The "Missing or Stale Data" Invariant
* **The Rule**: When an external API is down, rate-limited, or returns data older than 24 hours, the system **must never default to "Safe" or "Green"**.
* **Why**: In safety-critical systems, silence or missing data is not evidence of safety. Defaulting to "Safe" during an API blackout could send a family into a landslide zone.
* **Implementation**: The system explicitly flags the date with `level: -1`, `level_name: "Insufficient data"`, and tags the confidence as `"Insufficient data"`, advising travelers to verify with local authorities.

## 2. Future Weather & Hazard Prediction (The 4 Time Horizons)

### 2.1 The Problem
When a tourist plans a farmstay 2 days, 10 days, or 45 days in advance, how can the platform tell them what the weather and disaster risk will be? Do we collect sensor data to train an AI weather predictor?

### 2.2 The Scientific Reality & Our Architecture
No startup or single company can or should train its own atmospheric weather forecasting model from scratch. Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) requires global satellite telemetry, supercomputer clusters, and fluid dynamics solving systems (like ECMWF in Europe or NCMRWF in India).

AgroSafe Travel interfaces directly with **ECMWF / Open-Meteo High-Resolution Numerical Models**, **IMD Doppler radar & district bulletins**, and **NASA historical climatological baselines**. 

The future is divided into **4 scientifically calibrated planning horizons**:

```
[Target Booking Date]
       │
       ├─► 0 to 3 Days Ahead  ───► HORIZON A: High-Confidence Tactical Forecast
       │                           (Open-Meteo NWP + IMD Color Warnings + SACHET CAP 1.2)
       │                           Confidence: High | Zero-Fee Weather Cancellation Active
       │
       ├─► 4 to 7 Days Ahead  ───► HORIZON B: Medium-Confidence Operational Forecast
       │                           (Open-Meteo 7-day NWP + NASA IMERG Antecedent Soil Proxy)
       │                           Confidence: Medium | Advisory Guidance
       │
       ├─► 8 to 15 Days Ahead ───► HORIZON C: Extended Numerical Estimate
       │                           (Open-Meteo 16-day Ensemble Numerical Run)
       │                           Confidence: Low | Early Trend Estimate
       │
       └─► > 15 Days Ahead    ───► HORIZON D: Historical Climatological Baseline
                                   (NASA POWER 10-Year Climatology Baseline)
                                   Status: "Historical trend" / "Insufficient data"
                                   (Does not pretend to know daily weather months ahead)
```

---

### 2.3 Detailed Horizon Breakdown

#### Horizon A: 0 to 3 Days (Tactical Operational Window)
* **API Providers**: 
  - [Open-Meteo API](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/openMeteoAdapter.ts) (`https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast`): Fetches 24h precipitation sum, max/min temperature, relative humidity, wind gusts, and GloFAS river discharge percentiles.
  - [IMD Adapter](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/imdAdapter.ts): District-level 4-stage color warnings (Green, Yellow, Orange, Red) and 3-hour thunderstorm nowcasts.
  - [NDMA SACHET Adapter](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/sachetAdapter.ts): Real-time Common Alerting Protocol (CAP 1.2) XML feeds matched via ray-casting spatial point-in-polygon tests.
* **Confidence**: `"high"` (`horizon_label: "Forecast"`).
* **Booking Interaction**: If the calculated score or official alert reaches **Orange (2)** or **Red (3)**, the system unlocks the **Weather-Aware 100% Zero-Fee Rebooking & Cancellation Modal** on the traveler's dashboard ([`frontend/src/app/(site)/bookings/page.tsx`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/frontend/src/app/(site)/bookings/page.tsx)).

#### Horizon B: 4 to 7 Days (Mid-Range Forecast)
* **API Providers**: Open-Meteo 7-day numerical weather predictions + [NASA IMERG](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/nasaAdapter.ts) antecedent rainfall (3-day and 7-day cumulative precipitation to model soil saturation).
* **Confidence**: `"medium"` (`horizon_label: "Forecast"`).
* **User Value**: Gives tourists early signals to prepare appropriate gear (rain gear, warm layers, vehicle 4x4 checks).

#### Horizon C: 8 to 15 Days (Extended Ensemble Window)
* **API Providers**: Open-Meteo 16-day ensemble atmospheric run.
* **Confidence**: `"low"` (`horizon_label: "Early estimate"`).
* **User Value**: Numerical models beyond day 7 exhibit chaotic variance. The UI explicitly alerts the user that this is an early indicator subject to atmospheric shifts.

#### Horizon D: Beyond 15 Days (Long-Term Climatology)
* **API Providers**: [NASA POWER Adapter](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/powerAdapter.ts) (10-year monthly meteorological averages).
* **Confidence**: `"not_applicable"` (`horizon_label: "Historical trend"`).
* **The Honest Architecture Rule**: Commercial weather services that claim to forecast rainfall on a specific Tuesday 45 days in advance are misleading. AgroSafe Travel explicitly sets `level: -1` (`"Insufficient data"`) and displays the 10-year historical pattern:
  > *"Travel is 38 days away. Showing historical 10-year trend for month of November: averaged 2 heavy-rain days (NASA POWER). Automated daily re-checking will activate as travel date enters the 15-day forecast horizon."*

## 3. Physical Multi-Hazard Calculation Engines (Formulas & Logic)

The risk fusion engine ([`backend/src/services/riskEngine/fusion.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/riskEngine/fusion.ts)) evaluates five individual hazards for every farm and target date:

```
                                  ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                                  │      Weather & Static Input   │
                                  └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                                  │
         ┌───────────────────┬────────────────────┼───────────────────┬───────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                    ▼                   ▼                   ▼
   [ Landslide ]         [ Flood ]           [ Cyclone ]           [ Heat ]        [ Thunderstorm ]
   • Rain 24h          • River Discharge   • Coast Distance     • Max Temp        • IMD 3h Nowcast
   • Soil Saturation   • Rain 72h          • Wind Gusts         • Humidity        • Doppler Bulletin
   • Terrain Slope     • River Distance    • INCOIS Warning     • Rothfusz Index  • Severe Convective
   • Susceptibility    • CWC Alert Floor   • Sustained Gales    • IMD Heatwave      Advisory
         │                   │                    │                   │                   │
         └───────────────────┴────────────────────┼───────────────────┴───────────────────┘
                                                  │
                                                  ▼
                                      [ Multi-Hazard Fusion ]
                                 • Overall Level = MAX(Hazards)
                                 • Official Alert Floor Enforced
                                 • Freshness Verification (≤ 24h)
                                 • Confidence Tagging by Horizon
```

### 3.1 Landslide Hazard Module ([`landslide.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/riskEngine/landslide.ts))
* **Scientific Formula**: Evaluates forecasted 24h rainfall against standard IMD precipitation categories, compounded by antecedent soil moisture and topographical slope from Copernicus DEM.
* **Rules**:
  - `rain_24h >= 204.5 mm` (Extremely Heavy Rain) OR (`rain_24h >= 115.6 mm` on wet soil with slope > 20°) ➔ **Red (3)**
  - `rain_24h >= 115.6 mm` (Very Heavy Rain) OR (`rain_24h >= 64.5 mm` on wet soil with slope > 15°) ➔ **Orange (2)**
  - `rain_24h >= 64.5 mm` (Heavy Rain) OR (wet soil on high-susceptibility slope) ➔ **Yellow (1)**
  - Otherwise ➔ **Green (0)**
* **Official Floor**: If NDMA SACHET or Geological Survey of India (GSI) has an active landslide warning in the district, the score cannot be lower than that warning.

### 3.2 River & Flash Flood Module ([`flood.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/riskEngine/flood.ts))
* **Scientific Formula**: Ingests GloFAS (Global Flood Awareness System) river discharge percentiles combined with 72h cumulative precipitation and proximity to riverbed:
* **Rules**:
  - GloFAS River Discharge `percentile >= 95` (20-year flood event) OR (`percentile >= 85` within 1.0 km of river) ➔ **Red (3)**
  - GloFAS River Discharge `percentile >= 80` OR (`rain_72h >= 150 mm` within 2.0 km of river) ➔ **Orange (2)**
  - GloFAS River Discharge `percentile >= 65` OR (`rain_72h >= 75 mm` within 3.0 km of river) ➔ **Yellow (1)**
  - Otherwise ➔ **Green (0)**
* **Official Floor**: Central Water Commission (CWC) flood advisories set the baseline floor.

### 3.3 Extreme Heat Module ([`heat.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/riskEngine/heat.ts))
* **Scientific Formula**: Implements the **National Weather Service (NWS) Rothfusz Multivariate Regression Formula** for Heat Index ($HI$ in °F, converted to °C):
  $$HI = -42.379 + 2.04901523T + 10.14333127R - 0.22475541TR - 0.00683783T^2 - 0.05481717R^2 + 0.00122874T^2R + 0.00085282TR^2 - 0.00000199T^2R^2$$
  *(where $T$ = temperature in °F, $R$ = relative humidity percentage)*
* **Rules**:
  - $HI \ge 54^\circ\text{C}$ ($130^\circ\text{F}$) OR $T_{max} \ge 45^\circ\text{C}$ (Severe Heatwave) ➔ **Red (3)**
  - $HI \ge 41^\circ\text{C}$ ($105^\circ\text{F}$) OR $T_{max} \ge 40^\circ\text{C}$ (Heatwave) ➔ **Orange (2)**
  - $HI \ge 35^\circ\text{C}$ ($95^\circ\text{F}$) OR $T_{max} \ge 37^\circ\text{C}$ ➔ **Yellow (1)**
  - Otherwise ➔ **Green (0)**

### 3.4 Cyclone & High Wind Module ([`cyclone.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/riskEngine/cyclone.ts))
* Evaluates farms within 50 km of coastal zones against wind gusts ($>90\text{ km/h} \rightarrow \text{Red}$, $>60\text{ km/h} \rightarrow \text{Orange}$) and INCOIS storm surge bulletins.

### 3.5 Thunderstorm & Lightning Module ([`thunderstorm.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/riskEngine/thunderstorm.ts))
* Direct low-latency passthrough for IMD Doppler 3-hour severe convective nowcasts.

## 4. Path B: Incident Photo Verification & Claim Alignment Engine

### 4.1 The Core Problem
When a user uploads a photo claiming *"The main bridge to the farm collapsed in a flash flood"* or *"Landslide blocked Highway 58"*, how does the platform prevent:
1. Malicious competitors uploading photos of disasters from other countries to trigger mass cancellations.
2. Users reusing old viral photos from the 2013 Kedarnath disaster.
3. Pranksters taking photos of dry roads and claiming they are flooded.

### 4.2 The 7-Factor Path B Credibility Engine
Located in [`backend/src/services/alertEngine/photoVerification.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/alertEngine/photoVerification.ts), the system computes an auditable **Credibility Score (0 to 100)** based on 7 cryptographic, geospatial, environmental, and AI vision signals:

| # | Signal Name | Max Points | Verification Mechanism | Why It Prevents Fraud |
| :-: | :--- | :-: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Reporter Verification** | **20 pts** | Database query against `bookings` and `users` tables. | Verified guests with active bookings in the district or registered hosts receive 20 pts. Unverified accounts get only 5 baseline points. |
| **2** | **Live In-App Camera Lock** | **10 pts** | Device hardware stream capture tag (`isLiveInAppCamera`). | Directly accessing the camera sensor hardware stream prevents selecting pre-downloaded images from the device gallery. |
| **3** | **GPS Spatial Match** | **20 pts** | Compares photo embedded EXIF coordinates against device hardware GPS using the **Haversine formula**. | Enforces a **1.5 km tolerance**. If an image was taken in Nepal or Switzerland and submitted as a local Uttarakhand incident, it receives 0 pts. |
| **4** | **Weather Context Corroboration** | **20 pts** | Ingests [NASA IMERG satellite rainfall](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/nasaAdapter.ts) for that exact coordinate over the past 72 hours. | If the user claims a "Flash Flood" or "Landslide", but satellites and weather stations logged 0 mm rain and dry soil, the claim is flagged (+5 pts instead of +20 pts). |
| **5** | **Official Alert Corroboration** | **10 pts** | Point-in-polygon intersection test against active NDMA SACHET / IMD alert shapes. | If the government has already issued an active alert in that sector, the claim receives full corroboration (+10 pts). |
| **6** | **Perceptual Hash (pHash) Fingerprint** | **5 pts** | Computes 64-bit perceptual hash of the image and checks against past incident report database. | Prevents duplicate, recycled, or viral photos from being repeatedly submitted. |
| **7** | **AI Vision Hazard Classifier** | **15 pts** | **[Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/alertEngine/aiVision.ts)** (with resilient fallback). | Analyzes image pixels to verify whether visual features (mud, rockfall, floodwaters, debris) align with the claimed hazard type, filtering out unrelated photos, selfies, and memes. |

### 4.3 Score Bands & The Human-in-the-Loop Moderation Queue
* **High Credibility ($\ge 75$ points)**: Priority fast-track to the top of the Admin Moderation Queue.
* **Medium Credibility ($50 - 74$ points)**: Standard Admin Moderation Queue.
* **Low Credibility ($< 50$ points)**: Flagged as suspicious; requires secondary human host verification before any broadcast.

**The Golden Safety Rule**: A citizen report **never broadcasts an emergency evacuation or auto-cancels bookings automatically**. It creates a verified incident ticket that appears in the **Admin Moderation Dashboard** ([`frontend/src/app/(site)/admin/page.tsx`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/frontend/src/app/(site)/admin/page.tsx)). The administrator reviews:
- The user's claimed category
- The photo evidence
- The 7-factor signal breakdown (GPS match delta, NASA rainfall corroboration, camera lock status, and AI Vision assessment)
- The admin clicks **Confirm** (which broadcasts an advisory) or **Reject** (which dismisses the report).

### 4.4 The AI Vision Classifier Architecture ([`aiVision.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/alertEngine/aiVision.ts))
* **Technology**: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision (via `@google/genai` official SDK and REST API with multi-model fallback chain).
* **Free Tier Model**: Generous 100% free tier (15 requests/min, 1,500 requests/day, 1M tokens/min) with zero credit card required.
* **Resilient Dual-Mode Design**:
  - If `GEMINI_API_KEY` is present in `.env`, it runs deep multimodal visual analysis.
  - If the key is not yet configured or the network is offline, it activates a safe **Heuristic Resilient Fallback**, ensuring the system never crashes during demonstrations or internet outages.
* **Strict Weight Invariant**: Capped at 15 points. It corroborates the physical signals, but **never acts as the sole judge**. Physical ground truth (GPS, NASA satellite rain, live hardware shutter) remains the anchor.

## 5. Disaster-Aware Booking, Hold & Escrow Architecture

Traditional booking engines (Airbnb, Booking.com) operate on purely commercial terms: non-refundable deposits, rigid cancellation policies, and seat race conditions during flash panics. AgroSafe Travel re-engineers this for volatile rural environments:

### 5.1 15-Minute Atomic Seat Inventory Hold
* **The Problem**: When bad weather clears, or during peak harvest festivals, multiple travelers attempt to book the last room simultaneously, leading to double bookings and payment disputes.
* **The Solution**: Implemented in [`backend/src/services/bookingService.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/bookingService.ts#L45-L88):
  1. When a user enters checkout, the system atomically reserves the capacity in the `availability` table (`status: "held"` with an expiration timestamp: `held_until = NOW() + 15 minutes`).
  2. If the user completes payment via Razorpay, the hold converts to `"confirmed"`.
  3. If the user abandons checkout or payment fails, an automated cron job ([`backgroundJobs.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/backgroundJobs.ts#L52)) running every 2 minutes automatically releases the hold back to `"available"`.

### 5.2 Weather-Aware 100% Zero-Fee Rebooking & Cancellation
* **The Problem**: Tourists often endanger their lives driving into mountain passes during landslides because hotels refuse to refund their money.
* **The Solution**:
  - If a farmstay's calculated risk or official government alert reaches **Orange (Level 2)** or **Red (Level 3)** within **72 hours of check-in**:
  - The booking dashboard automatically activates the **Weather-Aware Rebooking Guarantee** ([`bookingService.ts:L116`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/bookingService.ts#L116)).
  - **100% of cancellation and rescheduling penalties are waived**.
  - The guest can either reschedule to any date within 90 days for free, or receive a 100% refund from the platform escrow.

### 5.3 Disaster Escrow Financial Protection
* Guest payments are held in platform escrow.
* Funds are only released to the farm host 24 hours **after** the guest completes check-in and confirms their safety via the platform's **"I'm Safe"** button ([`officialAlerts.ts:L142`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/alertEngine/officialAlerts.ts#L142)).
* If a disaster cuts off access roads prior to arrival, the guest's money is safely refunded from escrow without disputes.

## 6. Resilience, Caching & Data Reliability Invariants

### 6.1 Grid Cell Caching (Conserving API Limits)
Himalayan valleys often host dozens of farmstays within a 5-kilometer radius. If 500 farms made individual weather API calls every hour, API rate limits would quickly be exhausted.
* **Implementation**: Implemented in [`openMeteoAdapter.ts:L31-L38`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/openMeteoAdapter.ts#L31-L38):
  ```typescript
  getGridCell(lat: number, lng: number): string {
    const latGrid = Math.round(lat * 10) / 10;
    const lngGrid = Math.round(lng * 10) / 10;
    return `${latGrid.toFixed(1)}_${lngGrid.toFixed(1)}`;
  }
  ```
* All farms within the same 0.1° (~11 km) grid cell share a single cached forecast run (cached for 30 minutes in memory and 6 hours in the `forecast_snapshots` database table).

### 6.2 External Outage Circuit Breakers & Fallback Resiliency
Every external adapter inherits from [`BaseAdapter`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/baseAdapter.ts) and logs each execution in the `source_runs` table:
* If Open-Meteo or IMD fails with a network timeout, the adapter catches the exception, logs a failed run, and falls back to **seasonal historical baselines** with an explicit warning note attached to the response.
* If data is older than 24 hours, the `riskFusionEngine` refuses to compute an optimistic score and returns `"Insufficient data"`.

## 7. Pinpoint Q&A / Defense & Evaluator Cheat Sheet

Here are precise, authoritative answers to the exact questions technical judges, evaluators, and system auditors ask about this platform:

---

### Q1: "Why don't you use a trained Machine Learning model like XGBoost, Random Forest, or a Neural Network to predict disaster risk?"
> **Answer**:  
> In life-safety critical systems, **interpretability, auditability, and legal liability** supersede black-box statistical correlation.  
> 1. If an ML model predicts "Green" and a tourist is trapped in a landslide, the model cannot explain its reasoning to an inquiry committee or an insurance auditor.  
> 2. Our deterministic rule engine is grounded in established scientific standards: IMD precipitation thresholds (64.5 mm, 115.6 mm, 204.5 mm), the NWS Rothfusz Heat Index regression equation, and GloFAS 20-year flood discharge percentiles.  
> 3. Every single output provides an auditable breakdown of reasons and sensor timestamps.  
> 4. Government alerts (IMD, NDMA SACHET) legally and ethically set an unbreachable floor that no algorithm is permitted to override.

---

### Q2: "If someone books a stay for 2 months from now, how do you know what the weather will be? Are you predicting that far ahead?"
> **Answer**:  
> **No, and any service that claims to predict daily weather 60 days in advance is scientifically invalid.**  
> Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) models (ECMWF/GFS) lose atmospheric skill past 14–16 days.  
> * Beyond 15 days, our platform switches to **NASA POWER 10-year monthly historical climatology**.  
> * It explicitly flags the risk level as `level: -1` (`"Insufficient data"` / `"Historical trend"`).  
> * It provides the traveler with contextual climatological baselines (e.g. *"Averaged 2 heavy-rain days in November over the last decade"*).  
> * As the booking enters the 15-day window, our automated background cron job ([`backgroundJobs.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/backgroundJobs.ts)) automatically promotes it to live numerical forecasts, and at 72 hours, locks in real-time IMD/SACHET warning coverage.

---

### Q3: "How do you know that a photo uploaded by a user actually shows what they claim, rather than a fake or an old picture?"
> **Answer**:  
> Through our **Path B 6-Factor Credibility Engine** ([`photoVerification.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/alertEngine/photoVerification.ts)):  
> 1. **EXIF vs. Live GPS**: The photo's internal GPS must match the smartphone's hardware GPS within a 1.5 km tolerance (Haversine formula). You cannot submit a photo taken elsewhere.  
> 2. **In-App Camera Lock**: The photo must be captured live through the in-app camera stream, blocking uploaded web downloads.  
> 3. **NASA Satellite Rain Corroboration**: If a user claims a "Flash Flood", we query NASA IMERG satellite rain for that coordinate over the past 72 hours. If it logged 0 mm of rain, the claim is flagged as suspicious.  
> 4. **Official Alert Corroboration**: Checks if NDMA SACHET or IMD already flagged that area.  
> 5. **Perceptual Hash (pHash)**: Blocks recycled viral disaster photos.  
> 6. **Human Moderation Gate**: The calculated score (0–100) routes to an **Admin Moderation Queue** where a human confirms or rejects the claim before any community notification is dispatched.

---

### Q4: "What happens if an external API (like Open-Meteo or IMD) crashes or goes offline?"
> **Answer**:  
> The system enforces the **Missing or Stale Data Invariant**:  
> 1. External calls sit behind circuit-breaker wrappers ([`baseAdapter.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/adapters/baseAdapter.ts)) with 8-second timeouts and run-health logging in `source_runs`.  
> 2. If data cannot be fetched or cached data is older than 24 hours, the system **never defaults to 'Green/Safe'**.  
> 3. It explicitly returns `level: -1` (`"Insufficient data"`), transparently warning the user that telemetry is unavailable and advising direct contact with local authorities.

---

### Q5: "What if your physical rule evaluates a farm as 'Green' (Safe), but the government IMD alert is 'Red'?"
> **Answer**:  
> The system enforces the **Official Alert Floor Invariant** in [`fusion.ts:L131-L135`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/riskEngine/fusion.ts#L131-L135).  
> The final risk level is calculated as:
> $$\text{Final Level} = \max(\text{Calculated Rule Level}, \text{Official Alert Floor})$$
> If the IMD or NDMA SACHET warning is Red (Level 3), the platform level is forced to **Red**, regardless of what our local equations calculated. Our rules can upgrade risk, but they can never downgrade an official government alert.

---

### Q6: "How do you prevent hotel or room double-booking during sudden weather panic or rush?"
> **Answer**:  
> Through our **15-Minute Atomic Inventory Hold** in [`bookingService.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/bookingService.ts#L45-L88):  
> 1. When a user begins checkout, the system atomically reserves the room in the `availability` table with a `held_until = NOW() + 15 minutes` timestamp.  
> 2. Competing requests see zero availability.  
> 3. If payment succeeds, it converts to `confirmed`.  
> 4. If the user closes the tab or payment fails, our background cron worker ([`backgroundJobs.ts`](file:///media/shashi/New-Volume/Agro-full/backend/src/services/backgroundJobs.ts#L52)) checks every 2 minutes and releases expired holds back into public inventory.

---

### Q7: "Where in the codebase can an AI/LLM model be added if we wanted to incorporate one?"
